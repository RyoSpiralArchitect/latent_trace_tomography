# Industrial thesis

## Product category

The plausible product is a vendor-independent audit plane for model behavior,
not a hidden-thought reader.

Organizations often cannot inspect weights or activations for the models they
actually deploy. They still need to know whether:

- a vendor update changed the endpoint's failure dynamics
- two vendors reach the same answer through behaviorally different response regimes
- a model moved toward a fragile shortcut while headline accuracy stayed flat
- an agent failure is more consistent with evidence loss, binding failure, or uncorrected cue reliance
- an endpoint is outside the calibration domain and should be escalated

Latent Trace Tomography would return an output-only consistency class relative
to OSS reference profiles, supporting perturbations, separately scored failure
predictions, and an explicit abstention. It would not claim that a closed
endpoint's internal susceptibility profile has been calibrated or directly
estimated unless a separate comparability bridge earns that statement. That
audit layer can sit above multiple vendors without requiring privileged model
access.

## Defensible assets

The durable asset is not a single classifier. It is the combination of:

1. a library of OSS dependency clusters with controlled selected-site intervention profiles
2. versioned, factorized projection panels
3. cross-dependency-cluster transfer with explicit abstention bookkeeping;
   open-set validation is a successor-only asset
4. longitudinal endpoint fingerprints separated from mechanism features
5. content-addressed evidence packets suitable for incident and compliance review

The OSS models act as calibration phantoms. Their independence is counted by
`dependency_cluster_id`, derived from learned-weight, teacher/distillation, and
merge ancestry; checkpoints, adapters, fine-tunes, prompts, and cases are
nested measurements. Their value grows as the susceptibility-profile library covers
more genuinely independent clusters, architectures, post-training methods,
tasks, and failure regimes.

## First product wedge

Version-drift auditing is a practical early wedge:

- establish a projection baseline for a production endpoint
- re-run a query-budgeted sentinel panel on a schedule or release event
- detect response-surface change
- report which OSS-referenced behavioral equivalence class is most consistent
  with the change under the frozen panel and calibration domain
- predict high-risk challenge cases before production rollout

This is useful even if the mechanism label remains coarse. The customer-facing
claim is about reproducible behavior and prospective failures.

The current repository is earlier than this product wedge: OSS-1 is
design-frozen, while its discovery authorization, final execution lock, and
private post-output sealed-evaluation authorization are unassigned. It has made
zero model and API calls and has exposed no OSS outcomes. The next step is to
assign a content-addressed discovery
authorization, not to add an adapter or call a model. That authorization may
permit only bounded site discovery; the count- and evidence-closed discovery
report must be content-addressed before a separate final lock can authorize the
remaining work.

The engineering pilot is exactly three dependency clusters with one
`semantic_anchor`, one `surface_cue_anchor`, and one `mixed_anchor` in each,
for nine model units. All three anchors within a cluster share architecture
family and tokenizer identity. This balance prevents those nuisance identities
from directly decoding the anchor, while keeping effective `n` at three.

The eventual prospective evidence packet also closes six independent
commitments—weak records and inventory, development key and slots, and held-out
key and slots—and recomputes both baselines internally only after authority
opening. Failed causal controls, site instability, and closure errors are
retained as audit evidence and produce `unresolved` or fail-closed results;
they are not omitted to make a report look conclusive.

The output-independent execution lock carries no output-derived authority
commitments. Instead, the authority supplies the exact regenerated
prompt/raw-output inventory and ledger declaring 16,416 causal-label,
behavior-fit, and sealed-test forwards with diagnostics still at zero, then creates three
private post-output fold authorizations. Each binds the raw `D_sealed_test`
2,880 weak and 1,152 challenge trials, derived keys, and an opaque fold surface.
Raw model/cluster identities stay evaluator-private; even development cluster
IDs are HMAC-pseudonymized before entering a worker realm.

The production coordinator accepts exactly three prediction commitments before
any reveal. Fold-run records carry no reveal bytes or nonces; only after every
commitment, bundle, prediction, redaction, and common-root check closes does the
coordinator invoke the exact four-field opening provider and independently
rederive its bytes. It validates each development set as the other two folds'
held-out material under fold-private cluster-ID rekeying, then averages the
candidate and each baseline equally over the three clusters. It chooses the
stronger baseline only at that global level and applies the frozen 10 percent
gate. A single-fold score is not a product result. Capability
IDs and no-sharing declarations are useful audit records but not physical
attestation; fold-local training/replay remains a P1 control. Until it is closed,
the defensible assurance is prediction locking. Diagnostics run only after the
three-commitment barrier.

The OSS calibration packet also preserves the ancestry-derived independence
graph, exact carrier bindings, raw causal outputs, and per-condition operation
ledgers. This supports reproducible incident review without claiming that a
hash is hardware attestation. A higher-assurance product can later add signed
runner attestations without changing the output-only customer claim.

A three-cluster run would be an engineering pilot.
Confirmatory evidence requires a fresh outcome-naive execution with at least
three development and three sealed confirmatory dependency clusters, subject
to power. API adapters remain downstream of those gates.

## Hard constraints

- Query budget is part of the scientific and economic objective.
- Provider nonstationarity must be time-blocked and fingerprinted.
- Safety layers, routers, RAG, and postprocessors are part of the observed
  endpoint mechanism unless independently isolated.
- Model identity and inference-stack fingerprints are strong confounds, not
  evidence of computation class.
- A blind claim requires an authority/auditor capability boundary. Same-process
  hash ordering supports prediction locking only.
- Distinct worker IDs are declarations, not isolation attestation; raw private
  authorization and model/cluster IDs must never enter an auditor realm.
- High-stakes deployment requires conservative coverage and a human escalation
  path.

## Non-product claims

The system does not reveal private chain of thought, reconstruct weights,
attribute a response to a specific hidden head, or prove that an endpoint has a
human-like cognitive state. Those claims are neither needed for the industrial
value nor supported by output-only evidence. The CT metaphor describes an
inverse-measurement ambition, not a known linear forward operator or exact
internal reconstruction.
