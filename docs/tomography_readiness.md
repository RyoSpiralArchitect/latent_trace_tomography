# When can we call this tomography?

English | [日本語](tomography_readiness.ja.md) · November 2, 2026 · [Evidence ledger](tomography_readiness.json)

**Today: a new weak observation separated two known OSS pairs; OSS controls narrowed a prospective answer set for one closed endpoint. Still missing: a blind demonstration that the output-only estimate measures a reproducible intervention-defined property on a previously unseen model lineage.** The [checkpoint and replay](../reports/weak_projection_checkpoint_20261102/README.md) establish the first statement, not the second.

### Define the claim before designing the detector

Choose an observation panel and decoder **Q**, an OSS calibration population **C**, an operational reference **S**, and a resolution fine enough to matter. For the small cue assay, S was the selected-site targeted-minus-null donor-transfer count divided by all 16 evaluation pairs, when at least one pair distinguished base from donor. The [frozen OSS-1](../protocols/oss_reliance_regime_v0/protocol.json) instead specifies three susceptibility components and a semantic-minus-cue contrast. Success on the smaller assay cannot substitute for the full contract.

What can survive projection into text? If two admissible references have the same observed orbit but different S, the instrument cannot assign distinct unique estimates to them from that orbit. The 40-text collision had labels 9/16 and 12/16: any one point has worst-case error at least 3/32 on that pair. This is a limit on that **finite observation**, not a statement that the full stochastic models are equal. A new Q may distinguish the pair. Showing that it does is instrument development, not yet proof that its estimates of S transfer.

### Readiness by gate

| Gate | Evidence today | What would change the claim; what to do otherwise |
|---|---|---|
| **Q — repeatable separation** | Last-digit contrast: SmolLM2 split on 5/5 eligible cells; GPT-2 split earlier on 6/6 far controls. These are known pairs, not held-out mechanistic labels. | Freeze on fresh contents, balance cue, answer map and ordering; show between-anchor differences beyond within-anchor repeat changes. A marker-only change is a format result. No stable split → retain the equivalence class for Q. |
| **R — causal reference quality** | Named-anchor, selected-site cue counts exist. Some complete panels had no distinguishable base/donor answers; that is not zero effect. | Separate site discovery and evaluation; bind checkpoints, donor/base strata, opportunities and sites. Compare targeted vs norm-matched null, self-sham, control sites and codebook controls; report site stability. For a **model-level reliance** interpretation, separately validate symmetric opportunity and cross-site/model comparability. Failure → unresolved reference, not a training label. |
| **I — resolution and identifiability** | The 40-text collision, and raw-identical 80-slot twins with opposite measurement states, falsify unique readouts for those panels. In the nine-anchor trial, six conditional brackets did not give one unconditional numerical offer. | Prespecify acceptable resolution and support; keep a known collision control. Report measurement-state coverage, numerical coverage among measurable targets, width and misses on the **same** targets as a behavior-blind interval. Withhold unscorable evidence; do not count “both possible” as identification. |
| **T — transfer across independent units** | The historical bracket contained 3/4 scorable values, missed the same target as the blind range, and failed its no-misses gate. There is no qualified continuous-transfer result. | Hold out entire learned-weight dependency clusters, not prompts, sibling adapters or nominal family names. Fit features, site-selection rules, baseline choices and support thresholds without held-target labels. Score against disjoint OSS intervention references. Report per-cluster errors and abstentions; if it fails, claim only local discrimination. |
| **P — prospective value beyond a shortcut** | The controls-only lock narrowed six all-OSS `{A,B}` sets to correct singletons. Law and control-copy also got 6/6. This tests chronology and prediction, not advantage over simple explanations. | Freeze outputs on genuinely discriminating held challenges **before reveal**. Report candidate vs law, copy, advisory and unfiltered OSS. Distinguish an empty disagreement stratum from a win. For formal OSS-1 evidence, also pass the separate three-fold prospective gate below. |
| **U — unknowns, calibration and drift** | The harness can abstain and has seen out-of-support inputs. A valid false-attribution rate, calibrated posterior and drift operating point have not been established. | A separate successor must freeze unknown/negative controls, coverage vs conditional risk, accepted error/width and version-shift alarm limits **before** new data. Evaluate unseen clusters and endpoint revisions; fall back to behavioral reporting outside support. Returning a broad interval everywhere is not a pass. |

**Go/no-go for the word.** Q alone supports an output audit. Use *Q-restricted, OSS-calibrated tomography on the named pilot* only when the reference qualifies (R), the returned sets have useful demonstrated resolution (I), the profile transfers under a blind whole-cluster holdout (T), and the predeclared prospective comparison passes (P). If one is missing, report the narrower observed result. Validated open-set attribution, probabilities or deployment alarms additionally need U; distributing a replay is not that validation.

The [research contract](research_contract.md) and [independent-unit definition](../protocols/oss_reliance_regime_v0/independent_unit_definition.json) remain authoritative. The checklist above does not change thresholds:

- Within OSS-1, the intended-route excess must be ≥0.25, self-sham preservation ≥0.90, absolute control-site effect ≤0.10, codebook competence ≥0.90, and the selected site must recur in ≥6/8 discovery-seed omissions. The selected-site precision interval is not uncertainty over model populations.
- For the named-unit pilot, leave-dependency-cluster-out MAE for `delta_specific = S_semantic − S_cue` must be ≤0.25. Its secondary anchor-display rule is macro AUROC ≥0.70, every fold >0.60. These are not criteria passed by the last-digit answer comparison.
- For [prospective scoring](../protocols/oss_reliance_regime_v0/prospective_scoring_contract.json), close **all three fold commitments** before any reveal. Average candidate and each of the two frozen baseline log losses equally over held-out clusters; the candidate must reduce loss by ≥10% relative to the better **global** baseline. Do not pick a different winning baseline per fold or omit abstentions. Raw Brier and accepted-slot coverage are secondary, not probability-calibration certificates.
- Three dependency clusters support an **engineering transfer pilot** at most. For confirmatory language, freeze power, risks and multiplicity on fresh data; use at least three development and three separately sealed confirmatory clusters, more if power requires. Prompts, codebooks and replicates cannot increase that unit count.

### The next small experiment: make the explanations disagree

This is a **proposal, not a frozen protocol or run authorization**. Keep the observation side cue-aligned. On three fresh synthetic dockets and two answer maps, repeat the three aligned controls from the latest run twice: 36 endpoint control slots if separately approved. Only as *held challenges*, (1) leave an exactly matching code unchanged and flip its advisory from PASS to FAIL; (2) leave a same-prefix far mismatch unchanged and flip its advisory from FAIL to PASS. Two repeats give 24 possible challenge slots. Never use these conflicts to select the matching OSS anchors.

1. Fix the texts, training-overlap checks, codebooks, repeat rule, exact-support rule and full case inventory before collecting outputs. Record the same challenge outcomes on OSS; the endpoint's remain unopened.
2. From endpoint controls only, require stable matches in ≥2 OSS lineages and retain all ties. Lock the matched-OSS and all-OSS sets alongside the law, **copy of the corresponding aligned control**, and advisory-only predictions. Publish the per-comparator **disagreement mask** before sending the endpoint challenges. With no support or no singleton disagreement with any simple predictor, record that result and stop there.
3. For a match/PASS with a FAIL advisory, law and (if previously correct) control-copy predict PASS; following the advisory predicts FAIL. The matched-OSS prediction is an empirical result to lock, **not** assumed to be FAIL. Score all prespecified cells; report wins, losses, ties, ambiguous sets and unscorable repeats, separately for each comparator's disagreement cells and by docket/codebook. Agreement with the cue-only predictor cannot exclude cue-copying.

A differential win here would justify another blinded behavioral test. It would not supply the missing OSS causal qualification or identify a proprietary circuit.

### Language we can defend

- **Now:** “On this named panel and endpoint revision as served, the controls narrowed the observed OSS set and correctly forecast six held answers. Two simpler baselines tied.”
- **Only after the relevant gates:** “Under Q and calibration C, this target is consistent with class R at the validated resolution; here is its coverage, error and held behavior.” For closed endpoints this remains a **conditional compatibility** statement. Internal truth requires independent vendor-side validation.
- **Not implied:** recovery of a unique hidden trace, layer or chain of thought; population-level reliability from three lineages; a guarantee that a vendor update preserves the decision process.
